# Hardware buying guide · external-drum leader

The quantities below cover one tabletop station, both arms and both triggers. Each arm uses **three XM430-W210-T** at J1, J2 and J4, and **five XL330-M288-T** at J3, J5, J6, J7 and J8. J1 drives the external faceplate directly through the XM430 horn. The separate rear idler sets are used only at J2 and J4.

**Fabricate 26 prints from 16 designs and 5 metal pieces from 2 designs.** The metal pieces are one mast and four internal sleeves. The installed screw count is **174 external mounting screws** including the four bench bolts, plus **16 factory horn-center retainers**. There are 22 loose nuts, 32 flat mounting washers, 6 factory horn thrust washers and 8 heat-set inserts. These quantities exclude spare kit hardware, cables and the fasteners already inside the servos. ## Order list

All dimensions are in mm. A machine screw is specified as diameter × pitch × **under-head length**. Every screw in this build seats on a flat underside. Ø refers to diameter and AF to width across flats. Keep the two different M2 × 6 machine heads, the two M2.5 × 7 machine heads, and the XL330 plastic taps in separate bags. Order quantities are installed counts. Subtract matching items already in your motor boxes.

**Bench bolt length.** Add 8 mm of printed foot below the recess, bench and backer thicknesses, two 1.6 mm washers, 5 mm for the nut and the desired thread protrusion. For 25 mm of bench and 2 mm of protrusion this gives 43.2 mm. Choose M6 × 45 for that stack and verify clearance underneath. The two upper mast bolts have just 0.8 mm nominal protrusion with the specified washers and nuts, so check engagement before tightening.

**Small-motor screw totals.** For both arms, buy or inventory **40 × 2×10** plastic tapping screws for the rear cases (32 at J3/J5/J6/J7 plus 8 at J8), **24 × 2×7** for the J3/J5/J6 horns, and **16 × 2×6** for the palms and triggers. One 2×10 head up to Ø3.7 × 1.7 fits the dimensions specified for all 40 rear-case locations. Check the actual head, profile and under-head length on a sample.

**Qualify one XL330 before ordering in bulk.** Each kit lists six PHS M2 × 6 TAP and ten PHS M2 × 8 TAP screws. The supplied 2 × 8 reaches only 0.5 mm beyond the rear clearance entrance when driven through these 3.0 mm pads. The old 2 × 12 callout was for the earlier 4.2 mm case pad. It projects 9.0 mm through the new pad and can bottom out. Use a 2 × 10 with a tapping profile matched to the supplied screw. [ROBOTIS kit contents](https://robotis.us/products/dynamixel-xl330-m288-t) · [XL330 drawings](https://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/dxl/x/xl330-m288/#drawings)

For the 7 and 10 mm lengths, one source for samples is **EJOT DELTA PT size 20, WN 5452 pan head**. The nominal Ø3.5 × 1.6 head fits the specified envelopes. Confirm the supplier's actual head/length tolerances and compare the thread and tip with the factory screw, especially if the plastic hole is already threaded. The tapered tip reduces full-thread engagement. Measure projection and available pilot depth on an unpowered motor and verify retention on one print. [EJOT supplier](https://www.ejot-atf.com/product/delta-pt/) · [Size 20 dimensions and pilot guidance](https://www.bulten.com/media/b5eby1nc/deltapt-version-250422.pdf)

**First fit.** The useful first purchase is one J1 XM430 with its factory horn, four of each J1 M4, M2.5 and M2 screw, four short inserts, and three M3 saddle bolts with three plain nuts. Add one J2 XM430/idle set and the J2 row quantities to check an entire shoulder pitch assembly. This catches horn fit, socket access, insert seating and loaded sag before duplicating both arms. Use one XL330 with the stock plastic horn to qualify the distal screws separately.

## Power and TTL wiring

The **XL330-M288-T takes 3.7–6.0 V, with 5.0 V recommended**. The **XM430-W210-T takes 10.0–14.8 V, with 12.0 V recommended**. Use separate motor power rails. A straight-through three-wire TTL cable joins its supply pins end to end. Keep the XM430 supply pin isolated from every XL330 connector. In the CAD joint order, the boundaries are J2–J3, J3–J4 and J4–J5 on each side. Plan separate bus branches or a harness with separate power injection and a shared ground reference for the TTL data. Verify polarity and the measured voltage at each connector before attaching motors. [XL330 electrical specifications](https://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/dxl/x/xl330-m288/) · [XM430 electrical specifications](https://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/dxl/x/xm430-w210/)

A ROBOTIS U2D2 can provide the USB-to-TTL communication link. Size the external current-limited supplies, distribution, hubs and leads for the intended simultaneous load. The older XC330-T288-T and the XL330-M288-T share the external mounting drawing. The two models have different voltage and torque ratings. Use the label on the actual motor to select its supply.
