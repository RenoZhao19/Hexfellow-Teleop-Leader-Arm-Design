# What to buy and where each screw goes

Quantities below build **one tabletop leader with two round shoulders, two seven-joint arms and two squeeze triggers**. They count installed pieces, including hardware supplied with motors. For one arm, use half of each shoulder, arm and grip row. The foot and mast are shared. For the bench sequence and labeled shoulder parts, open the [shoulder assembly guide](Shoulder_assembly.md).

All dimensions are in millimetres. Machine screws are listed by thread **diameter × pitch × length**. Length runs from the flat underside of the head to the tip. Head envelopes are **maximum outside diameter × height**. Each group takes a flat-seated head. Keep the five J1 fastener groups in separate bags while building.

**Table bolt example.** Add 8 of printed grip below the head recess, table thickness, backing plate thickness, both washers, nut height and the required thread protrusion. A 25 mm table, zero backing-plate thickness, two 1.6 washers, a 5 mm nut and 2 mm protrusion give 43.2 mm. Choose **M6 × 45** for that example and verify the space underneath. Total for a through-bolted bench plus mast is **8 M6 nuts and 16 M6 washers**.

**Small-screw ordering shortcut.** For the stock XC330 plastic, the combined tapping-screw totals are **32 × 2×12, 24 × 2×7, 8 × 2×10 and 16 × 2×6**. Use a head ≤Ø3.7 × 1.7 for a single 2×6 SKU in both grip locations. Compare the factory PHS TAP screw with a supplier sample for pitch, profile, point and head before committing to the 7, 10 and 12 mm lengths. The ROBOTIS drawing gives a 4.5 mm long entrance before the narrower rear pilot; the depth available beyond it for the point must be measured on a purchased servo. The tip reaches farther than the full-diameter thread. A longer screw can bottom inside the case even if the head looks seated. [ROBOTIS XC330 drawings](https://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/dxl/x/xc330-t288/#drawings)

**Where to source the nonstandard tapping lengths.** Start with the ROBOTIS PHS TAP supplied in the servo box as a thread sample. For the 7, 10 and 12 mm lengths, one supplier to request samples from is EJOT ATF. Specify **DELTA PT size 20, WN 5452 pan head**, plus the under-head lengths in the table. Its nominal Ø3.5 × 1.6 head fits all three designs on paper. Ask for tolerances and compare a sample with the factory thread before installing in a servo, especially one with previously formed threads. The generic family drawing does not establish stock for those exact lengths or a ROBOTIS-qualified replacement. Measure the actual screw projection, particularly the 2 × 7 horn screw with only 0.2 nominal margin. [US supplier inquiry](https://www.ejot-atf.com/product/delta-pt/) · [Head, length tolerance and pilot guidance](https://www.bulten.com/media/b5eby1nc/deltapt-version-250422.pdf)

**First purchase for a fit check.** For just the round shoulder, buy or borrow one XM430 with its factory horn, two 6805ZZ bearings, one circlip and the **per-shoulder** amounts in J12–J16. Make one spindle and spacer pair. Check shaft fit, ring seating, insert retention, screw projection and hand rotation on that cartridge before duplicating the parts. For the distal arm, one XC330 and one set of tapping screw samples establish the actual rear pilot depth, head fit and driver access. The printed parts and machining files are in the [parts inventory](CAD_parts_and_quantities.csv). Some CAD entries represent a group of four heads as one occurrence. Use the physical quantities on this page for ordering.
