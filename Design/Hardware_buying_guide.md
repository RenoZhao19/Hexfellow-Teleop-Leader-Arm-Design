# Hardware buying guide · external-drum leader

The quantities below cover one tabletop station, both arms and both triggers. Each arm uses **three XM430-W210-T** at J1, J2 and J4, and **five XC330-T288-T** at J3, J5, J6, J7 and J8. J1 drives the external faceplate directly through the XM430 horn. The separate rear idler sets are used only at J2 and J4.

**Fabricate 26 prints from 16 designs and 5 metal pieces from 2 designs.** The metal pieces are one mast and four internal sleeves. The installed screw count is **174 external mounting screws** including the four bench bolts, plus **16 factory horn-center retainers**. There are 22 loose nuts, 32 flat mounting washers, 6 factory horn thrust washers and 8 heat-set inserts. These quantities exclude spare kit hardware, cables and the fasteners already inside the servos. ## Order list

All dimensions are in mm. A machine screw is specified as diameter × pitch × **under-head length**. Every screw in this build seats on a flat underside. Ø refers to diameter and AF to width across flats. Keep the two different M2 × 6 machine heads, the two M2.5 × 7 machine heads, and the XC330 plastic taps in separate bags. Order quantities are installed counts. Subtract matching items already in your motor boxes.

**Bench bolt length.** Add 8 mm of printed foot below the recess, bench and backer thicknesses, two 1.6 mm washers, 5 mm for the nut and the desired thread protrusion. For 25 mm of bench and 2 mm of protrusion this gives 43.2 mm. Choose M6 × 45 for that stack and verify clearance underneath. The two upper mast bolts have just 0.8 mm nominal protrusion with the specified washers and nuts, so check engagement before tightening.

**Qualify one XC330 before ordering a bulk pack.** The 2 mm plastic screw callouts give diameter and under-head length. ROBOTIS identifies its included PHS TAP but does not give a steel machine-thread pitch for these plastic pilots. Each motor's packing list includes six PHS M2 × 6 TAP and ten PHS M2 × 8 TAP. Use the factory screw to match the thread profile, pitch, tip and head. The 2 × 8 gives only 0.5 mm into the narrower J8 rear pilot and exceeds the J8 horn depth by 1 mm. [ROBOTIS supplied screws](https://www.robotis.com/shop/item_export_only.php?it_id=902-0171-000) · [XC330 drawings](https://emanual.robotis.com/docs/en/dxl/x/xc330-t288/#drawings)

For the 7, 10 and 12 mm lengths, request a supplier sample such as **EJOT DELTA PT size 20, WN 5452 pan head**. Nominal head Ø3.5 × 1.6 fits the listed envelopes. Compatibility with ROBOTIS plastic and existing formed threads, point depth, actual length tolerance and availability at these lengths need supplier and physical confirmation. Measure projection through the real print and receiving depth on an unpowered motor. Check retention on a spare before purchasing all 80 screws. The guide does not establish a qualified substitute or tightening torque for these taps. [EJOT supplier](https://www.ejot-atf.com/product/delta-pt/) · [Size 20 dimensions and pilot guidance](https://www.bulten.com/media/b5eby1nc/deltapt-version-250422.pdf)

**First fit.** The useful first purchase is one J1 XM430 with its factory horn, four of each J1 M4, M2.5 and M2 screw, four short inserts, and three M3 saddle bolts with three plain nuts. Add one J2 XM430/idle set and the J2 row quantities to check an entire shoulder pitch assembly. This catches horn fit, socket access, insert seating and loaded sag before duplicating both arms. Use one XC330 with the stock plastic horn to qualify the distal screws separately.

**Powered bench setup.** A ROBOTIS U2D2 provides the USB-to-TTL communication link. Supply motor power separately through suitable distribution with adjustable current limiting. The overlapping published input range of these two T-series motors is **10–12 V**. Size the supply, each distribution branch, hub and cable for the intended simultaneous current. Match the three-pin TTL ports and harnesses to the actual parts. One bench adapter does not supply motor power or implement the force-feedback controller.
