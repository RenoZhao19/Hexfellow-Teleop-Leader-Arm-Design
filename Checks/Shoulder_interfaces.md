# External drum and shoulder interfaces

The torso holds the fixed XM430 case. The J1 horn drives the faceplate, the detachable saddle and the entire J2 assembly. The faceplate clears the drum rim. The rim supplies no outboard bearing support. These are measurements of the nominal CAD solids in the [geometry manifest](geometry_hashes.json).

| Interface | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed drum to rotating faceplate | Axial extents measured along the J1 axis, relative to the torso face | Drum ends at 51.50 mm and the faceplate starts at 53.00 mm. The 1.50 mm gap is independent of J1 angle. |
| Horn screws to fixed assembly | Swept cylinders enclose the four M2 screw shanks and heads at every J1 angle. Checked against the drum, case, four case screws, flange screws and inserts | Zero intersecting volume with each fixed occurrence. The drive horn is the intended thread receiver. |
| J2 moving assembly to fixed drum | Checked all six J2 moving CAD components against a coaxial Ø88 mm cylinder enclosing the drum, from axial −20 to +51.5 mm. Subdivided J2 motion from −70° to +125.003°. Each bound subtracts the maximum possible displacement from the mid-interval exact distance and a 0.02 mm margin. Rotating about J1 preserves distance to the cylinder. | At least 3.283 mm for every J1 angle across this J2 interval. The upper fork gives the smallest bound. [Component results](J2_to_drum_clearance.csv) |
| Adjacent shoulder groups, including the rotating saddle | Checked 106 left-shoulder component combinations affected by the new parts at 1,320 J1/J2 settings. J1 ran −160° to +160° in 10° increments and J2 ran −70° to +125° in 5° increments. Both arms use identical shoulder parts. | The vendor case/horn seam touched in all 1,320 settings. Its exact common volume was zero at three representative settings. No other pair produced a surface crossing in this grid. This grid samples adjacent links. |
| Fixed-in-group interfaces | Exhaustively intersected the ten pairs in group 0 and 28 pairs in group 1 on one shoulder at nominal fit | Two positive volumes occur where smooth screw models enter the vendor tap cores. They are 16.0245 mm³ for the four J1 case screws together and 7.2550 mm³ for the four horn screws together. All other pairs had zero common volume to the reported precision. [Pair results](fixed_pair_intersections.csv) |
| Motor and tool access | Inserted the fixed XM430 at six offsets along the drum axis. Checked the case and flange driver passages. With the rotating saddle detached, checked four screw-head paths for the J2 rear case mount, three saddle-key paths against the cradle and J2 hardware, and four screw-head paths through the J1 faceplate. | Nominal rigid access envelopes cleared. The saddle and cheek installation order controls tool access. |
| Torso and post | Intersected the torso with the shortened post, upper sleeves, upper pins and six installed J1 components per side. Checked the print volume around all eight rear insert seats. | Zero unintended solid overlap. The modeled plastic surrounds an annulus out to Ø10.5 mm through the first 4 mm of each seat. |

The shoulder screen checks rigid parts. A prototype is needed to measure rim clearance under hand load, bearing play at the XM430 output, insert retention, fastener engagement and cable movement. Cable loops, connector latches and tool handles require a hands-on clearance check before powered testing. The [full station checks](Collision_and_workspace.md) also identify arm postures with actual rigid interference.
